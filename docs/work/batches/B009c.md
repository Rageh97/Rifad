## Behavior

- `ordering-contract` exports `ORDERING_EVENT_TYPES`, typed payloads `CheckOpenedV1`, `CheckRoundSubmittedV1`, `ItemVoidedV1` and `CheckCancelledV1`, and `validateOrderingPayload(type, payload)`. It rejects unknown types and malformed fields (UUIDs, round number, 1–500 lines, quantities 1–999, decimal prices with ISO currency, reason-code format, preparation state).
- The envelope already carries tenant, branch, check ID, `aggregateVersion`, correlation and occurrence time (§25: correlation + aggregate version/sequence + per-consumer ordering).
- Published versions never change shape; a breaking change is a new `.v2` type.
- The [event catalog](../../catalogs/event-catalog.md) now lists the four events with plane, ordering, producer, payload contract and data classes.
- Domain alignment: `addLine` now rejects non-UUID product IDs (ADR-017), so every produced payload passes the validator.
