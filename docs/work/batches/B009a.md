## Behavior

- **States (§9.1):** `OPEN → CLOSING → CLOSED`, `OPEN → CANCELLED`, and `CLOSING → OPEN` only when finalization is abandoned before completing. CLOSED is final; corrections go through a refund or credit note (G03).
- **Channels:** dine-in, takeaway and delivery share the aggregate; dine-in needs a table reference.
- **Lines:**
  - Each line carries its sale-time `PriceSnapshot`, checked against product, currency and channel; the snapshot never changes afterwards.
  - Quantity is 1–999. Only lines not yet sent can change or be removed.
- **Rounds:** `submitRound` sends all unsent lines as round N+1 and the check stays open for more rounds (ADR-021). It emits `restaurant.check_round.submitted.v1` with the round number and line snapshot.
- **Voids:** a sent line needs a controlled reason code (UPPER_SNAKE) and a manager approver, and records actor and preparation state. It emits `restaurant.item.voided.v1`; the line stays in history.
- **Cancellation:** after any round was sent it needs a reason and a manager approver. It emits `restaurant.check.cancelled.v1`.
- **Closing:** `beginClosing` requires no unsent lines and at least one billable line. `closeCheck` requires a `FinalizationReceipt` for this check (ADR-039), so Ordering cannot close a check on its own.
- **Events and totals:** every change increments `version`, and events carry it as `aggregateVersion` (ADR-033). `checkTotal` sums non-voided lines exactly in minor units. Splitting payment by items belongs to Payments.

## Risk review

Abuse cases: rewriting sent orders (sent lines immutable; voids audited by reason, actor and approver), price tampering after sending (frozen snapshot), voids or cancellations without approval (rejected), closing without payment and invoice (finalization receipt required), silent reopen (impossible). Residual: who counts as a manager is enforced by the permission pipeline when the API is built (B009b); the reason-code list and cancellation charges are still open (G03); currency minor units assume SAR-style 2 decimals (KSA-first, ADR-019).
